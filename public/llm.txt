# RNVCO

> RNVCO is a unit of Rycoon building a 2.4 GWh sovereign AI data centre network across India. Phase 1 launches with 400 MWh capacity. The platform is engineered for data sovereignty, Tier 4-style facilities, pan-India low-latency coverage, and DPDP Act 2023 / MEITY compliance.

## Overview
- **Name:** RNVCO
- **Parent Organization:** Rycoon
- **Core Industry:** Sovereign AI Infrastructure, GPU Compute, Data Centres
- **Target Network Capacity:** 2.4 GWh total
- **Primary Website:** https://rnvco.com
- **Contact:** hello@rnvco.com

## Deployment Roadmap
- **Phase 1 (Active / Launching):** 400 MWh Foundation Layer capacity.
- **Phase 2 (Planned):** 1.0 GWh Scale Layer expansion.
- **Phase 3 (Planned):** 1.0 GWh Dominance Layer network completion.

## Key Features & Value Proposition
- **Sovereign AI Compute:** Keeps sensitive data and AI model training inside Indian borders.
- **Data Compliance:** Fully compliant with DPDP Act 2023 and MEITY regulations.
- **Tier 4 Standard Infrastructure:** Liquid immersion cooling, high-density GPU racks, and sub-10ms latency grid across major hubs (Hyderabad, Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai).
- **Target Audience:** Enterprise Clients, Investors, Government / PSU Bodies, Technology Partners, and AI Research Institutions.
